WHO WE ARE

Arcane Learning is a collection of original browser games built from scratch using vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas.

Every game here is hand-coded — no engines, no frameworks, just pixels and code.

THE GAMES

  • CITY RACER — Open world city driving. Survive as long as you can. ⚠ Currently unplayable at school — blocked by Linewize.
  • SEA OF PHANTOMS — Wave shooter. Defend the pier from the undead.
  • LAST STAND — Tower defense. Hold the line against the AI.
  • POLYISLAND — Smoothie tycoon. Grow fruit, blend smoothies, build an empire.
